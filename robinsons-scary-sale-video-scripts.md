## What the research says (why the scripts look like this)

### 1. Robinson's own Meta ads: the clearest lesson
| Ad | Type | Click rate | Cost per website click |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlet – Modular Sectional | product + price photo | **1.50%** | ~$0.32 |
| Outlet – Outdoor Poly Rocker | product + price photo | **1.13%** | $0.34 |
| Outlet – Top Grain Leather | product + price photo | **1.09%** | $0.31 |
| Main – La-Z-Boy Recliners $399 | product + price photo | 0.76% | $0.30 |
| Outlet – We Pay Sales Tax | general offer banner | 0.52% | — |
| Outlet – Save Big on Closeouts | general offer banner | 0.48% | — |
| **60th Anniversary Main Store** | **video** | 0.49% | **$10.70** |
| **60th Anniversary Outlet** | **video** | 0.79% | **$8.56** |

- **The 60th Anniversary videos lost people almost immediately.** About 19,800 people started the Main Store video. Only 336 (under 2%) made it a quarter of the way through, and the average watch time was **2 seconds**. Each website click cost about 30× more than the photo ads.
- **A specific product with a specific price wins.** Those ads got 2–3× the clicks of general "sale" banners.
- **So every video below opens on a real couch and a real price in the first 2 seconds, with a person in frame.** No logo intro and no "thank you for 60 years" opening.

### 2. What works in this space (outside research)
- **Show a person in the first 2 seconds.** TikTok's analysis of ads found:
  - 90% of an ad's recall impact happens in the first 6 seconds.
  - A person on screen early made ads about 50% better at hooking viewers.
  - Someone talking straight to camera was linked to +128% purchase intent.
- **Meta rewards a specific style.** Its analysis of Reels ads found the best performers shared four traits: vertical 9:16, sound on, a real person, and text on screen with a lo-fi look. Those ads got 56% higher click rates and 20% cheaper clicks.
- **Lo-fi beats polished.** When Fantastic Furniture (a furniture chain) tested do-it-yourself creator-style videos against cut-down TV spots, the creator-style videos won on both click rate and cost per click.
- **The owner as the recurring face is a proven local model:**
  - Mattress Mack (Gallery Furniture, Houston) built a brand on the owner talking fast to camera with one catchphrase.
  - Bob's Discount Furniture's founder starred in deliberately unpolished ads.
- **Caution:** a New Hampshire furniture owner's "help us stay open" video went hugely viral, and the store then closed with 50+ complaints. Keep the energy fun and confident. Don't use "going out of business" or desperation framing.
- **Make everything work on mute.** Burn captions into every video and put the offer, dates and address on screen as text.

### 3. Rules for every video
1. **Shoot vertical (9:16).** Keep each video 15–30 seconds, and also cut a 6–8 second version for Stories.
2. **Within the first 2 seconds:** a face, a couch and a price.
3. **Captions on everything.** The price and the date "NOW THRU NOV 2" also go on screen as text.
4. **Keep text out of the danger zones.** Stay out of the top ~15% and bottom ~35% of the screen, where Reels buttons cover it.
5. **End on a clear call to action:** store name, address, "Now thru Nov 2".
6. **Halloween is the seasoning, not the meal.** The couch and the price are always the hero.

## The scripts
Film **every script at both stores**, swapping in the address. Each script has **two opening hooks**. Film both, so we can test which one keeps people watching.

### SCRIPT 1 — "The Scream" (15s) ⭐ lead ad
**Why:** A face plus a price in the first second gives a surprise reaction. It's the simplest test of the hook.

| Time | Shot | Audio / text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook A:** tight on a staffer's face. They flip a price tag over, then scream. **Hook B:** start ON the price tag ($529), then pull back to the shocked face | 🔊 "AHHHH!" · TEXT: **$529 SOFA?!** |
| 2–5s | Staffer turns to camera, calm | "Sorry. Our prices are just *that* scary right now." |
| 5–11s | Fast cuts: 3 couches with their tags (Stonemeade $529 → Gardiner $719 → Foreside $739) | "Sofas from five twenty-nine. Up to fifty percent off." |
| 11–15s | Staffer waving from a couch, with a small pumpkin prop | "Now through November 2nd. Robinson's, [Third Street / Conner Road], Oxford." · TEXT: **NOW THRU NOV 2** + address |

### SCRIPT 2 — "Owner Pitch" (25–30s) ⭐ trust ad
**Why:** The owner as the face is the proven local model, and talking straight to camera builds purchase intent.
**Who:** the owner or the most recognizable family member. Use a lav mic.

| Time | Shot | Audio / text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook A:** owner sitting on the Stonemeade, pats the cushion. **Hook B:** owner holding the $529 tag up next to their face | "This whole sofa? Five twenty-nine." |
| 2–8s | Owner walking past 2–3 couches (on a gimbal or walking backward) | "We've got to make room on the floor, so these prices are honestly a little scary." |
| 8–18s | Price-tag close-ups cut in while they talk | "Brett Honey, nine seventy-nine. Brogliano leather, ten forty-nine. And the Bucktown sectional with the ottoman, thirty-three ninety-nine." |
| 18–25s | Back to the owner, close | "We've been family owned here in Oxford since 1966. Come sit on them yourself." |
| 25–30s | End card over the owner | "Now through November 2nd." · TEXT: **PRICES SO LOW IT'S SCARY · NOW THRU NOV 2** + address/phone |

### SCRIPT 3 — "Couch Countdown" (20–25s) ⭐ matches the carousel
**Why:** It mirrors the carousel graphics. Each couch gets 3 seconds, and the price is always on screen.

| Time | Shot | Audio / text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook A:** jump-cut straight onto the Bucktown sectional (flop onto it). **Hook B:** close-up of the hand flipping the $529 tag | "Six couches. Six scary-low prices. Go." |
| 2–20s | 3 seconds per couch: wide shot → sit → tag. TEXT = name + price, big | Stonemeade $529 · Gardiner $719 · Foreside $739 · Brett Honey $979 · Brogliano $1,049 · Bucktown $3,399 |
| 20–25s | Staffer to camera | "Which one are you taking home? Now through November 2nd." · TEXT: address |

*Only include couches that are actually on that store's floor.*

### SCRIPT 4 — "The Sit Test" (15s)
**Why:** A real person sitting down is the furniture version of a product demo. It shows comfort, which a photo can't.

| Time | Shot | Audio / text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook A:** someone drops backward onto the sofa in slow motion. **Hook B:** close-up of the hand pressing into the cushion | TEXT: **The $529 sit test** |
| 2–9s | Real reaction, stretches out, puts feet up (Foreside recliner: show the recline) | "Okay… this is way too comfy for five twenty-nine." |
| 9–15s | Pull back to show the full set + tag | "Prices so low it's scary. Robinson's, now through November 2nd." |

### SCRIPT 5 — "Haunted Showroom" (15s) — fun/organic
**Why:** Gives a light Halloween touch for a scroll-stopping, shareable post. Good as an organic post or a cheap test.

| Time | Shot | Audio / text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Lights dim, staffer holding a flashlight under their chin on a couch | (spooky whisper) "They say if you sit on this couch at midnight…" |
| 3–8s | Beat. Lights snap on, big grin | "…you'll realize it only cost five twenty-nine." |
| 8–15s | Quick price cuts + wave | "Happy Halloween from Robinson's. Now through November 2nd." |

### SCRIPT 6 — Outlet "Warehouse Walk" (20s) — Outlet only
**Why:** The Outlet's product-plus-price ads were the account's best performers. This shows the volume and the deals.

| Time | Shot | Audio / text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook A:** staffer bursts through the warehouse doors. **Hook B:** a slow push down an aisle with a price tag in the foreground | "Welcome to the scariest prices in Oxford." |
| 2–15s | Gimbal walk, stop at 4–5 pieces, tags in frame | "Sofas, sectionals, recliners, bedroom sets, dining… up to fifty percent off." |
| 15–20s | Staffer at the door | "The Robinson's Outlet, 101 Conner Road. Now through November 2nd." |
