# Robinson's Furniture — "Prices So Low It's Scary" video scripts
Filming day: both stores

**Main Store:** 406 S Third St, Oxford PA · 610-932-3600
**Outlet:** 101 Conner Rd, Oxford PA · 610-932-6100 · Mon–Fri 10–7, Sat 10–5, Sun 12–5

> ⚠️ Confirm with the store before filming: are the couch prices below still right for 2026? Is it still "up to 50% off"? Are free local delivery and interest-free financing (from the Labor Day sale) still offered? Remove any line that isn't true.

Film everything **vertical (9:16)** for Reels/Stories. Grab a few horizontal B-roll clips too. Keep each script under 30 seconds. The first 2 seconds have to stop the scroll.

---

## 1. "The Scream" (15s, both stores, main hook ad)
**Shot:** Tight on a staffer's face. They look down at a price tag, then their eyes go wide.

- **STAFFER (screams, horror-movie style):** "AHHHH!"
- *Cut to the tag: Stonemeade Sofa, $529*
- **STAFFER (calm, to camera):** "Sorry. The prices at Robinson's are just… *that* scary right now."
- **STAFFER:** "Sofas starting at five twenty-nine. Up to 50% off. Come see it before it's gone."
- **End card:** Prices So Low It's Scary · [store address] · robinsonsfurniturepa.com

*Film it once at each store with that store's address on the end card.*

---

## 2. "Couch Countdown" (25–30s, Main Store)
**Shot:** Staffer walks the floor holding a small jack-o'-lantern. Quick cut at each couch.

- **STAFFER:** "Six couches. Six prices so low they're scary. Let's go."
- *Gardiner Sofa Chaise:* "Gardiner Sofa Chaise. Seven nineteen."
- *Stonemeade:* "Stonemeade sofa. Five twenty-nine."
- *Foreside:* "Foreside sofa. Seven thirty-nine. Recliner, four thirty-nine."
- *Brett Honey:* "Brett Honey. Nine seventy-nine."
- *Brogliano leather:* "Brogliano leather. One thousand forty-nine."
- *Bucktown Sectional:* (drops onto it) "And the Bucktown sectional with ottoman? Thirty-three ninety-nine. For the WHOLE family."
- **STAFFER:** "Robinson's Furniture, Third Street in Oxford. Don't be scared, come on in."

*If any of these couches isn't on the floor, skip it. Just film the ones you can actually show.*

---

## 3. "Warehouse Closeout" (20s, Outlet)
**Shot:** Start outside on the Outlet sign. Push through the doors into the warehouse floor.

- **VO / STAFFER:** "Something scary is happening at the Robinson's Outlet…"
- *Slow pan across the rows of furniture*
- **STAFFER:** "Our Warehouse Closeout Sale! Up to 50% off sofas, bedrooms, mattresses, dining, everything on the floor."
- **STAFFER:** "Once it's gone, it's gone. 101 Conner Road, Oxford."
- **End card:** Outlet hours + 610-932-6100

---

## 4. "Haunted Living Room" (15s, fun/organic, either store)
**Shot:** Staffer sits on a couch with a bowl of candy. Lights dim slightly.

- **STAFFER (spooky whisper):** "They say if you sit on this couch at midnight…"
- *Beat. Normal voice, big grin.*
- **STAFFER:** "…you'll realize it only cost five twenty-nine. Happy Halloween from Robinson's!"

*Swap in any couch and its price. Film 2–3 versions.*

---

## 5. "Owner / Family" (30s, trust ad, Main Store)
**Shot:** Owner or long-time staffer, seated, natural light, store floor behind them.

- **OWNER:** "We've been selling furniture in Oxford since 1966. Sixty years."
- **OWNER:** "And this is one of the best times of year to buy. Our prices are so low it's scary. Sofas start at five twenty-nine, and we've got deals in both stores."
- **OWNER:** "Come see us on Third Street, or out at the Outlet on Conner Road. We're family owned, and we'll take care of you."

---

## 6. Couch spotlights (6 × 10s, one per couch)
**Shot:** Slow 360° walk around each couch, then sit and show it's comfy, then hold on the price tag.

- **STAFFER:** "[Name] sofa. [Price]. Prices so low it's scary, only at Robinson's."

*These cut straight into individual ads, so each couch can run its own video next to its graphic.*
