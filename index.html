Areas of Expertise

What We Help With

Every child presents differently. We focus on understanding the "why" behind the struggles—not just labeling symptoms.

You may not have a clear diagnosis—and that's okay. We help you understand what's really going on.

Attention & Focus

Struggling to stay on task, finish homework without constant reminders, or manage time and responsibilities.

Anxiety

Constant worry, avoiding school or social situations, needing reassurance, or feeling overwhelmed by everyday things.

Autism Spectrum

Difficulty with social interactions, rigidity with routines, sensory sensitivities, or challenges with communication.

Depression & Mood

Low mood, irritability, pulling away from family or friends, or losing interest in things they used to enjoy.

Executive Functioning

Trouble getting started, staying organized, following through, or managing daily tasks independently.

Learning Differences & Twice-Exceptional

Bright, capable children who seem like they should be doing well—but struggle with reading, writing, math, or inconsistent performance.

Complex or Unclear Presentations

When multiple challenges overlap—and it's hard to understand what's really going on or where to start.

If you're unsure what your child is struggling with, we can help clarify that.

Our Process

A Clear, Thoughtful Process

We aim to make this process structured, collaborative, and easy to understand from the very beginning.

1
Initial Free Consultation

We begin by listening carefully and understanding your concerns. This conversation helps determine whether an evaluation makes sense—and if so, which type would be most helpful.

2
Comprehensive Evaluation

We ask parents, teachers, and when appropriate, your child, to complete rating scales in advance. The evaluation typically lasts 2 to 2.5 hours — integrating all information into a clear, actionable plan.

3
Collaboration & Ongoing Care

Care does not happen in isolation. We collaborate closely within our team, and when helpful, with therapists, schools, and other providers—ensuring alignment and continuity.
